# Product Principles

**Status:** TARGET_SPEC  
**Implementation:** PARTIAL — Phase 1 boundaries and persistent identities only

1. **Motivation and consistency first.** Progression should make adherence feel meaningful.
2. **Progress must be credible.** Time alone is not an earned achievement.
3. **No infinite difficulty escalation.** Same-level cycles refresh the experience without deliberately making every cycle harder.
4. **No unsafe acceleration.** Extra workouts do not shorten eligibility windows.
5. **Availability is user-owned.** Weekly frequency never increases because the system thinks the user advanced.
6. **Earned progress is durable.** Inactivity does not demote level or erase completed cycles.
7. **Inactivity is not progress.** Long pauses freeze active-time accumulation.
8. **Renewal is not promotion.** Motivational renewal refreshes a program but does not masquerade as an achievement.
9. **Safety overrides rotation.** Limitations and equipment compatibility constrain every substitution.
10. **Explain every meaningful change.** Users should know why a program changed and what changed.
11. **Preserve origin.** Starting level remains historical even when current training level advances.
12. **Separate career from program.** Re-prescription can restart a program period without erasing level/cycle history.
13. **XP is global.** Workout lifecycle never hardcodes XP economics.
14. **Performance claims require performance data.** Until actual reps/RPE/RIR or equivalent signals exist, progression is based on active time and adherence, not inferred strength gains.
