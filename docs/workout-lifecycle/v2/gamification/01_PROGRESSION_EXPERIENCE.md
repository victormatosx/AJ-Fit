# Progression Experience

The user should clearly feel that consistency produced progress without exposing rigid internal timers. UI may combine progress bar, valid workouts completed, consistency quality, “next evolution approaching” states and current training level/cycle context.

Avoid presenting a simple `73/90 days` countdown as the core mechanic. Internal timing may be flexible and paused by inactivity. User-facing progress should emphasize actions and consistency.

Earned evolution deserves a celebratory transition; motivational renewal should feel encouraging but not equivalent to a trophy.
