# Unlock Experience

Earned evolution should open a dedicated celebratory experience: acknowledge consistency, explain why the change happened, summarize what changed, and preview the new program. Level Promotion should feel more significant than a same-level cycle refresh.

The user may activate immediately or defer under the two-plus-two-week rule. Future XP/achievement presentation can be attached through Gamification events.

Motivational renewal uses a supportive refresh experience, not “you leveled up” language.
