**Status:** TARGET_SPEC
**Implementation:** IMPLEMENTED — `src/i18n/resources/{pt-BR,en,es}/evolution.json`. No raw threshold copy (28/85 days, 70%/85%) exists anywhere in the namespace; proximity/unlock/motivational-renewal copy keys follow this doc's tone directly. See architecture/11_PHASE_5_IMPLEMENTATION.md.

Use language of consistency, journey, completion and readiness. Explain that the prior program “completed its role” rather than calling it obsolete or bad.

Earned evolution: celebratory and causal — the user's consistency unlocked the next stage. High-adherence achievements may receive stronger recognition.

Motivational renewal: encouraging and forward-looking — “let's refresh your program and regain rhythm”; never present it as an earned level-up.

Avoid exposing exact hidden thresholds in a way that encourages gaming or unsafe extra training.
